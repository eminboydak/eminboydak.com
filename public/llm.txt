# emin boydak

> Emin Boydak is a systems engineer based in Istanbul, Turkey. He works on forward
> deployment engineering, embedded systems and full-stack development, and writes about
> systems, AI agents, TTRPG and field work. He is the founder of Stancona.

This site is bilingual. Turkish is the default locale at `/`; English lives under `/en/`.
